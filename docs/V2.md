## 1. Why (measured on 11-11-2026)
- The engine is sound (`engine/` 7,039 lines, tests 7,760). The gap is all in what the customer sees and takes away.
- Every tool opens empty: dashes and "5 questions left" (DSCR), 3 (CMA), 6 (estimate), 13 and a blank sheet (report). Inputs on the page at 1280 px: DSCR 35, report 33, CMA 131, estimate 26.
- The report's Check step is 5,138 px tall; the result sits in a column of about 435 px, the paper beside it at about 9 px type; one typeface, one navy, no accent, no chart.
- The report's files: PDF 9 pages, Helvetica not embedded, "Rs.", no chart or logo. Excel 10 sheets, 0 formulas, 0 names, 0 frozen panes, 0 charts. Word: no pictures.
- The process: `docs/DECISIONS.md` 90,224 words, `docs/GOTCHAS.md` 13,703, `scripts/site-check.mjs` 2,086 lines; in a month 303 commits changed docs files 892 times and `site/src` 628; 26 design reviews by our own agents; no outside user; four plans in three days.
- The article Rahul sent (Mollick on Fable 5.2) names the same trap: a rule book of 87,500 words, work "accurate and joyless", critics who never asked whether it was a slog. The fix there was an outside look at the whole process.

## 2. What version 2 is
1. **Opens alive**: each tool opens on a worked example, stamped "Example", that the customer types over. Nothing of theirs is assumed (rule 14 holds).
2. **Answer in the first minute**: three or four inputs give the first result; the rest starts from a trade's usual shape (a dated, sourced data file) or is read from the borrower's papers, each for them to confirm.
3. **The finding leads**: "Your business can carry ₹…" in the customer's words; the method one tap down.
4. **Handles on the result**: loan, rate, years and sales as sliders on the result itself; one chart kit for screen, PDF and Word.
5. **A design language**: a display face, a text face and a figures face (each with a Devanagari companion), one accent, green and red only for meets and falls short, the answer across the full width.
6. **One file** for a business, feeding all four papers (FILE1).
7. **Papers that decide on page 1** (§5).

## 4. Rules that still bind, and traps
- Numbers only from the engine (rule 12); the sketch on Rahul's page used the worked example's figures read from the site. Nothing from Rahul's office (D-BIZ-02); no bank-internal material (rule 19); never aimed at the employer bank's customers (rule 20); never "architect" (rule 21).
- An example is not a default: it is stamped, and the customer's result stays provisional until their own figures replace it.
- LOOK1's first step: keep `noindex` on `/v2/` and make the v1 site check skip `/v2/` (its page list), so v1's field counts and design checks do not gate v2.
- The `lender-documents` skill's "no PDF library" is set aside for PAPER1 (rule 2): the library loads only on the Download tap. Pick it by a short bake-off on the report's page 1: embedded fonts with ₹, Devanagari shaping, vector charts, size.
- Papers are made on the device. Printing on our server (the easy road to Hindi) is not taken: it breaks "nothing leaves your device".
- The line "can carry Rs. 29,98,000" followed by "No more can be borrowed" reads as a contradiction today: v2 says what fits and why in one sentence.
