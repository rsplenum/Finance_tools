# Finline's project report, as the owner met it (20-10-2026)

The owner made an account on finline.in and built a PMEGP report for a fictional "Tripathi Traders" (handicrafts, Bharatpur), recording the screen. Read from the two recordings frame by frame; figures as shown on screen. A competitor's page, not a source for any rule or rate.

## What it asks (10 steps, about a minute)
1. Name of the business. 2. Kind of business (free text). 3. Loan: Mudra, PMEGP or ordinary MSME. 4. Purpose: term loan, working capital, or both.
5. "What all do you need?": ticks with an amount each (land, shed/building, machinery, computers, furniture, electrification, racks, transport, installation, other). The owner gave land 50 L, shed 25 L, machinery 50 L, furniture 10 L.
6. A read-back: project cost Rs 1,35,00,000; "your investment (margin money) 10%" Rs 13,50,000; eligible loan Rs 1,21,50,000.
7. Monthly expense: ticks with amounts (rent, salary, raw material 50 L a month, power, phone, marketing and others).
8. Working capital wanted: 50 L. 9. Owner: name, gender, education, social category, when started. 10. Business: address, village or town, PIN, type of registration, phone, email.
Then a sign-up wall (Google, or name, phone, email and password) before anything is shown.

## What it gives
- A dashboard: "Your report is ready", "100% complete and eligible to submit for a bank loan", a "100% compliance" seal, nine "What you told us" cards each with Edit, a section checklist, and claims ("accepted by SBI, HDFC, PNB, ICICI, Axis", "2.75 lakh+ reports").
- A preview of about 25 sections (at a glance, feasibility ratios and graph, introduction, products, process, market, competitive advantage, future plans, CSR, project cost, working capital, sales, expense, application and means of finance, profit and loss, cash flow, balance sheet, repayment, DSCR, depreciation, assumptions, conclusion, break-even), with item names and every other year shown as `****` until payment.
- Prices: Lite Rs 499 (up to 3 years of projections, 7 pages, loans under 2 lakh); Pro Rs 999 (up to 10 years, 20+ pages); for professionals Rs 2,750 for 3 months (25 reports) or Rs 6,500 a year (100 reports).

## Where it goes wrong (what ours must not do)
1. **Sales invented.** No sales question; the report shows 1,950 units at Rs 3,403.85 for 6 months (Rs 3,98,25,030) and builds the DSCR (average 3.93, "a stable venture") on it.
2. **Subsidy both ways.** The text says the borrower "is eligible to receive margin money subsidy"; the application of funds shows 0.00 % on every line and the cash flow 0.
3. **The scheme's limits unchecked.** A Rs 1.9 crore PMEGP project including land. The owner recalls PMEGP's cap and its land rule; to be checked against KVIC's guidelines before any rule is written.
4. **Own share on the wrong base.** 10 % of the fixed cost (13.5 L of 135 L); with working capital the project is 190 L and the promoter's 18.5 L is 9.7 %.
5. **Break-even empty.** Fixed cost blank, break-even 0.00 % and Rs 0.00.
6. **Narrative made up.** A business not yet started is "reputable and well-established… strong market presence and loyal customer base", with CSR work with NGOs.
7. What holds: two DSCR years recomputed by hand match (27-28: (78.17 + 9.10 + 11.51) / (20.18 + 11.51) = 3.12; 29-30: (95.54 + 6.88 + 6.56) / (25.13 + 6.56) = 3.44).

## Tradeoffs put to the owner (each with its recommendation)
- Speed or truth: keep "never default a missing fact", but ask sales as Finline asks costs (one tick and amount per product); say "every figure is yours or a stated assumption".
- Scheme checks from dated data files (PMEGP, Mudra, CGTMSE: cap, land, own share, subsidy), shown beside the report.
- The answer before any sign-up; sign-up only to save or download.
- Narrative only from the user's inputs, marked draft, no claims a new business cannot have.
- Their prices (Rs 999 a report, Rs 6,500 a year for professionals) as the benchmark when P1d sets ours.
