- **The compaction size** (D-TECH-23): sessions compact at about 192k tokens (window 260000). Replayed on the long V1 and L1 session, every size from 144k to 216k saved about 26 to 32%, and larger sizes paused less: each compaction stops the work about 2 minutes, about 15 minutes an hour of work at 144k and 9 at 192k. 100k would compact every 4 to 8 steps, pause 33 to 65 minutes an hour and could cost more than not compacting. For another size, set the window to the size ÷ 0.8 + 20000 in `.claude/settings.json`; to have it in every repo, add `CLAUDE_CODE_AUTO_COMPACT_WINDOW` as a variable in the cloud environment's settings (the environment menu in the session's title bar, then Edit). After two or three one-feature sessions, run the script in the `lessons` skill to check the size.
