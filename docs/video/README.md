# The owner's video inputs (D-TECH-56, D-TECH-57)

The owner's own design inputs for the video production system, kept here until `rsplenum/video-studio` exists (VID1 moves them there).

- `pipeline-prompt.md`: the eight-phase pipeline prompt ("OpusDirector"). D-TECH-56 says which phases VID1 builds and what replaces Hyperframes, Parakeet and the audio connectors.
- `looks-catalogue.md`: the 40 looks. Looks 1 to 20 follow WeAreNoCode's free "20 Looks for Claude Motion"; 21 to 40 are fusions of them. Home's intros use look 1, Tech Glassmorphic, with marigold as the one accent (D-UX-124).
- WeAreNoCode's PDF itself is not kept in this public repo (it is their work, with no licence to republish); VID1 adds it to the private video-studio repo.

The motion reviews are under `docs/design-reviews/`: `2026-10-22-motion-reference.md` (the reference short), `2026-10-22-motion-analysis.html` (the first-principles analysis) and `2026-10-22-motion-review.html` (an outside review of HOME1c's script and the visual QA workflow).
