# CLAUDE MOTION DESIGN LOOKBOOK: 40 PRODUCTION STYLES

This reference guide provides 40 ready-to-paste design specifications for AI-driven video engines, Claude Motion, Remotion, and Hyperframes.

---

## HOW TO USE THESE LOOKS

When prompting Claude to create or animate a scene, structure your input using this formula:

```text
Role & Goal: [Who you are and what the video is communicating]
Audience: [Who will watch it]
Key Data / Script: [The exact copy, metrics, and takeaways]
Look: [Paste one of the Look blocks below]
Call to Action: [The final scene / next step]
```

# PART 1: THE ORIGINAL 20 LOOKS

### 1. Tech Glassmorphic
- **Best for:** A product walkthrough for a software tool, or an IT firm explaining a new service.
- **Look:** Deep navy background (`#0B1020`) with two slow-drifting blurred glows, teal (`#2DD4BF`) and violet (`#8B5CF6`), behind frosted glass panels: 24px rounded corners, white at 8% opacity, background blur, a thin white edge. Text in white (`#F8FAFC`) and soft gray (`#94A3B8`). Headings in **Sora SemiBold**, large and tight. Body in **Inter Regular**. Numbers count up inside glass cards with a soft teal glow behind them. Charts are thin glowing lines and translucent bars that grow from the bottom. Panels float up into place with a gentle overshoot, then fade out. Scenes change with a focus pull: the old panels blur away as the new ones sharpen. Camera drifts slowly forward with parallax between layers.
- **Avoid:** Neon overload, busy patterns, text on a glow with no panel behind it.

### 2. Swiss Grid
- **Best for:** A quarterly results summary from an accounting firm or a consultant.
- **Look:** Flat off-white background (`#F4F1EA`) on a strict 12-column grid with wide margins. No texture, no gradients. Black (`#111111`) for type, one signal red (`#E63322`) for the key number, light gray (`#C8C4BB`) for rules. Headings in **Inter Tight Bold**, very large, flush left, tight line spacing. Body in **Inter Tight Regular**, small. Graphic elements: thin horizontal rules, solid circles and rectangles, numbered section markers (01, 02, 03). Numbers sit huge and flush left, counting up in red. Bar charts are flat black bars on hairline axes. Motion is crisp and mechanical: elements slide in along the grid with a fast ease-out and stop dead. Scenes change with a hard horizontal wipe. Camera stays locked.
- **Avoid:** Shadows, rounded corners, centered text, a second accent color.

### 3. 3D Isometric Miniature
- **Best for:** A real estate market update, or a property manager's area report.
- **Look:** A 3D isometric miniature world on a soft sky gradient (`#FCE7C8` to `#F9B98B`), like a tabletop model at golden hour. Low-poly buildings and streets in warm cream (`#FFF4E0`), terracotta (`#D9734E`), and sage green (`#8FB996`), with long soft shadows and a light haze. Headings in **Outfit SemiBold**, body in **Outfit Regular**, on small floating white labels. Numbers appear as price tags or pins that pop up above buildings and count up smoothly. Charts are 3D blocks rising out of the ground like towers. Objects drop in from above with a soft bounce, one after another. Scenes change by gliding to another part of the same world. Camera holds the isometric angle, glides slowly, and orbits a few degrees.
- **Avoid:** Flat 2D icons, harsh black outlines, cold blue light.

### 4. Editorial Magazine
- **Best for:** A financial advisor's market commentary, or a law firm's monthly client briefing.
- **Look:** Warm paper background (`#F5EFE6`) with very faint grain, like a printed feature spread. Ink black (`#1A1A1A`), oxblood (`#7A1F2B`) for accents, gray-brown (`#8A8178`) for captions. Headlines in **Instrument Serif**, huge, with one word set in italic. Body in **Libre Franklin Regular** in narrow columns. Graphic elements: thin column rules, drop caps, pull quotes in large italic, small all-caps section labels with wide letter spacing, page numbers. Numbers look like a magazine stat: big serif figures with a short caption under them. Charts are thin ink lines with labels written right on them. Text reveals line by line through a soft upward mask, unhurried. Scenes change like turning a page, a slow horizontal slide. Camera makes slow pushes into the headline.
- **Avoid:** Bright colors, icons, bouncy motion, sans-serif headlines.

### 5. Neo-Brutalist
- **Best for:** A loud offer announcement for a gym, an agency, or a trade business running a promo.
- **Look:** Flat backgrounds that alternate between lemon yellow (`#FFE24A`) and white (`#FFFFFF`), no gradients. Every shape has a thick 4px black (`#000000`) border and a hard offset shadow, 8px down and right, zero blur. Accents in hot pink (`#FF5CA8`) and electric blue (`#3B5BFF`). Headings in **Archivo Black**, oversized, uppercase. Body in **Space Grotesk Medium**. Graphic elements: chunky rectangles, stickers, fat arrows, starbursts. Numbers sit in boxed tiles and snap to the final value with a quick shake. Bar charts are thick bordered blocks that slam up. Motion is fast and punchy: elements drop in with a hard snap, buttons press down into their shadow. Scenes change with a hard cut or a full-color block wipe. Camera is static.
- **Avoid:** Soft shadows, thin fonts, slow fades.

### 6. Newsroom Data Desk
- **Best for:** A bookkeeper's monthly client report, or an insurance broker explaining a rate change.
- **Look:** A clean white background (`#FFFFFF`) with a pale gray panel behind each chart, like a data story on a news site. Text in near-black (`#121212`). One highlight color, deep blue (`#1F5FBF`), for this month, orange (`#E8710A`) for warnings, gray (`#B5B5B5`) for everything else. Headlines in **Libre Franklin ExtraBold**, sentence case, written as a one-line takeaway. Body in **Source Serif 4**. Labels sit right on the chart, no legends. Numbers tick up quickly and land with a short annotation line. Lines draw left to right, bars grow in order, and the key bar turns blue last. Motion is calm and quick, ease-out, no bounce. Scenes change with a fast fade through white. Camera stays still and zooms gently into the part that matters.
- **Avoid:** 3D charts, decoration, crowded pie charts.

### 7. Soft Clay 3D
- **Best for:** A welcome video for new patients at a dental clinic, or new families at a tutoring service.
- **Look:** Soft pastel background (`#F3EEFF`) under gentle studio light, no texture. Every object looks like smooth matte clay: rounded, puffy, with a soft inner highlight and a blurred contact shadow. Palette: lilac (`#C9B6FF`), peach (`#FFC4A3`), mint (`#A8E6CF`), with text in deep plum (`#3B2A5C`). Headings in **Baloo 2 ExtraBold**, round and friendly. Body in **Nunito SemiBold**. Graphic elements are chunky 3D icons, pill buttons, and small rounded tiles. Numbers sit on clay badges and count up with a squishy pop. Bar charts are rounded clay pillars that grow with a soft wobble. Elements pop in with a springy squash and stretch, then settle. Scenes change as objects bounce out and new ones roll in. Camera slowly orbits a few degrees.
- **Avoid:** Sharp corners, thin lines, dark backgrounds.

### 8. Blueprint
- **Best for:** A contractor's project walkthrough, or a home inspector's report summary.
- **Look:** Deep blueprint blue background (`#0D3B66`) with a fine white grid at 10% opacity and a faint paper texture. All lines in white (`#FFFFFF`) and pale blue (`#A9D6F5`), with safety yellow (`#FFD23F`) only for the thing that matters. Headings in **Barlow Condensed SemiBold**, uppercase, widely spaced. Notes in **Architects Daughter**, like hand-lettered annotations. Graphic elements: technical line drawings, dimension lines with arrows, callout circles, a title block in the corner with a drawing number. Numbers appear as measurements on dimension lines and count up. Charts are plotted as lines on the grid. Everything draws itself on, stroke by stroke, at a steady drafting pace. Scenes change by zooming into a detail callout that becomes the next drawing. Camera pans slowly across the sheet.
- **Avoid:** Filled shapes, gradients, cute icons.

### 9. Kinetic Type
- **Best for:** A 30-second promo for a new service, or the opening of a sales presentation.
- **Look:** The words are the animation. Solid backgrounds flip between jet black (`#0A0A0A`) and signal yellow (`#FFD400`), with white (`#FFFFFF`) type. Headings in **Anton**, uppercase, filling the frame edge to edge. Supporting lines in **Barlow Medium**, small. No icons, no illustration. Words slam in one at a time on the beat, stretch and stack. Key numbers fill the screen and count up fast. Charts are made of type: a bar is a word stretched to length. Motion is fast with hard eases, 2 to 4 words per second, a short hold on the punchline. Scenes change with a wipe that reveals the next scene through the letters. Camera makes quick punch-ins and small rotations.
- **Avoid:** More than 6 words on screen, soft fades, thin weights.

### 10. Bento Dashboard
- **Best for:** A marketing agency's monthly results, or a property manager's update for owners.
- **Look:** Light cool gray background (`#F2F4F7`) with rounded tiles (20px corners) in different sizes. Tiles in white (`#FFFFFF`) with a very soft shadow, one hero tile in deep ink (`#101828`) with white text. Emerald (`#12876A`) for good news, coral (`#F04438`) for bad news. Headings in **Geist SemiBold**, numbers in **Geist Mono**. Each tile holds one idea: a big number, a sparkline, a progress ring, or a short bar list. Numbers roll up like an odometer. Sparklines draw left to right, rings fill clockwise. Tiles arrive one by one with a short stagger, scaling up from 96%. Scenes change when one tile grows to fill the screen and becomes the next scene. Camera is flat and still.
- **Avoid:** More than 8 tiles, 3D, heavy shadows.

### 11. Risograph Zine
- **Best for:** An event recap for a café, a local shop, or a community workshop.
- **Look:** Off-white uncoated paper (`#F7F3EA`) with visible grain, like a two-color print. Inks: fluorescent pink (`#FF48B0`) and teal (`#0078BF`), with yellow (`#FFE800`) where they overlap. Ink layers sit a few pixels off, with rough edges. Headings in **Bricolage Grotesque ExtraBold**, chunky and a little uneven. Body in **Courier Prime**. Graphic elements: halftone dot fills, cut-out shapes, stars, rubber-stamp marks. Numbers are big, printed in pink over a teal halftone, and step up in visible jumps. Bar charts are halftone blocks. Motion runs at 12 frames per second for a handmade feel: things pop, jitter slightly, and stamp into place. Scenes change by sliding a new sheet of paper over the old one. Camera is a flat overhead view.
- **Avoid:** Smooth gradients, gloss, perfect vector edges.

### 12. Retro Terminal
- **Best for:** An IT provider's monthly report, or a security check-up for a small business.
- **Look:** A black CRT screen (`#050805`) with faint scanlines, a soft green glow, slight screen curve, and a gentle flicker. Text in phosphor green (`#33FF66`), dim green (`#1A7A33`) for secondary lines, amber (`#FFB000`) only for warnings. Headings in **VT323**, large. Body in **IBM Plex Mono**. Graphic elements: a blinking block cursor, command prompts, boxes and progress bars drawn with text characters. Text types on character by character at a fast, steady pace, and some lines print all at once. Numbers count up like a loading counter. Charts are text-character bar charts that fill block by block. Scenes change with a screen clear: a quick flash, a scanline sweep, then a fresh prompt. Camera stays locked with a slow push-in.
- **Avoid:** Any color besides green and amber, rounded cards, bouncy easing.

### 13. Paper Cut-Out
- **Best for:** A how-it-works explainer for an accountant's new service, or a nonprofit's year in review.
- **Look:** Layered paper craft, built from stacked sheets of colored card with real depth. Each layer casts a soft shadow on the one below, with rough edges and a fine paper fiber texture. Palette: warm cream (`#FAF3E3`), forest green (`#2F5D50`), mustard (`#E0A526`), brick red (`#B5462E`), ink navy (`#1F2A44`) for text. Headings in **Fraunces SemiBold**, soft and slightly quirky. Body in **Work Sans**. Graphic elements: cut-paper hills, suns, buildings, folded corners. Numbers are printed on paper tags that flip up and count. Charts are stacked paper strips that slide up. Layers slide in from the sides with a soft ease, and pop-up pieces fold upward, like a hand-built set. Scenes change as a new paper layer slides across. Camera moves sideways with parallax.
- **Avoid:** Gloss, neon, thin lines.

### 14. Black and Gold Luxury
- **Best for:** A listing presentation for a high-end home, or a private wealth review.
- **Look:** Deep black background (`#0A0A0A`) with a subtle warm vignette and very fine noise. Gold (`#C9A55C`) for lines, numbers, and accents, with a soft metallic sheen sweeping across them. Warm ivory (`#F2EBDD`) for text, charcoal (`#1E1E1E`) for panels. Headings in **Cormorant Garamond Light**, large, with generous letter spacing. Small labels in **Montserrat**, uppercase, widely spaced. Graphic elements: hairline gold frames, thin dividers, a monogram-style emblem, plenty of empty space. Numbers fade in and count up slowly, then a light glint passes across them. Charts are thin gold lines with small dot markers. Motion is slow and deliberate, long ease-in-out, nothing bounces. Scenes change with a slow fade through black. Camera makes slow pushes in.
- **Avoid:** Bright colors, fast cuts, heavy bold fonts, crowded layouts.

### 15. Topographic Map
- **Best for:** A neighborhood report for an agent, or a territory update for an insurance broker or a delivery business.
- **Look:** Off-white map paper (`#EFEBE0`) with light brown contour lines (`#C9B79C`) across the frame. Water in muted blue (`#9CC3D5`), parks in sage (`#B7C9A8`), roads in white with thin gray edges, and one route or zone color, vermilion (`#E2522B`). Headings in **Josefin Sans SemiBold**, uppercase and widely spaced like map labels. Place names in **Lora Italic**. Graphic elements: pins, dashed routes, a compass rose, a scale bar. Numbers sit in callout pins that drop onto the map and count up. Charts are zones that fill with color by value, like a heat map. Routes draw themselves along the roads. Scenes change by flying to a new part of the map. Camera pans and zooms smoothly across one big map.
- **Avoid:** Satellite imagery, 3D buildings, app-style cards.

### 16. Whiteboard Sketch
- **Best for:** Walking new clients through a process, like how a claim works or the steps of buying a home.
- **Look:** A clean whiteboard (`#FAFAF7`) with faint smudges near the edges. Marker colors: black (`#1B1B1B`) for most lines, blue (`#1E5AA8`) for labels, red (`#D7263D`) for circles and emphasis, green (`#2E8B57`) for good outcomes. Headings in **Caveat Bold**, body in **Patrick Hand**, both like quick handwriting. Graphic elements: hand-drawn boxes, wobbly arrows, plain icons (houses, coins, documents, clocks), underlines, check marks. Lines have a slight hand wobble. Numbers are written stroke by stroke, then circled in red. Charts are sketched bar charts with hatching. Everything draws itself on at writing speed, as if a marker is moving live. Scenes change with a quick eraser swipe, or by sliding to an empty part of the board. Camera is flat, with small zooms.
- **Avoid:** Perfect geometric shapes, solid fills, drawn people.

### 17. Memphis Playful
- **Best for:** A fun promo for a party rental business, a bakery's new menu, or a salon's referral offer.
- **Look:** Bright cream background (`#FFF6E5`) scattered with geometric patterns: squiggles, zigzags, confetti dots, triangles, half circles, and small grid patches. Palette: hot coral (`#FF6B6B`), turquoise (`#2EC4B6`), sunflower (`#FFC93C`), and black (`#1A1A1A`) for outlines and text. Headings in **Bungee**, blocky and loud. Body in **Rubik Medium**. Shapes have thin black outlines, and some carry black dot or stripe patterns. Numbers sit inside bold shapes and pop up with a spin. Charts are colorful blocks and pie wedges with patterned fills. Motion is playful and springy: shapes spin in, bounce, wiggle once, and settle, while the background patterns float slowly. Scenes change with a burst of shapes flying across the screen and clearing it. Camera is flat with small playful zooms.
- **Avoid:** Muted colors, serif fonts, realistic shadows, cartoon characters.

### 18. Y2K Chrome
- **Best for:** A launch teaser for a beauty brand, a boutique, or a service for creators.
- **Look:** Pale icy background (`#EEF0FF`) with a faint lilac haze and soft bokeh sparkles. Hero elements are glossy liquid chrome: silver (`#C0C7D1`) with bright white highlights and iridescent edges shifting between baby blue (`#9AD0FF`) and lilac (`#D7B8FF`). Deep navy (`#0E1A3A`) for text. Headings in **Unbounded Bold**, wide and bubbly. Body in **Space Mono**, small. Charts are glossy rounded bars with reflective tops. Elements float in with a gentle bob and turn in 3D to catch the light. Scenes change with a liquid chrome morph or a sparkle flash. Camera drifts and tilts slightly.

### 19. Archival Documentary
- **Best for:** A company history for an anniversary, or the founding story of a family business.
- **Look:** A dark wooden desk (`#2B2420`) covered in aged paper. Paper in yellowed cream (`#E8DCC2`), ink in sepia brown (`#4A3728`), stamps in faded red (`#9E2A2B`), accents in tarnished brass (`#A68A4D`). Headings in **Libre Baskerville Bold**. Body and dates in **Special Elite**, like a typewriter. Graphic elements: typed index cards, file folders, stamps that thump down, a timeline drawn as a thread between pinned cards, and empty photo frames. Numbers are typed on, then stamped. Charts look hand-ruled in ink on graph paper. Motion is slow: pages slide onto the desk, dust drifts, the light flickers softly. Scenes change by sliding to the next document. Camera makes slow pans and pushes across the desk.
- **Avoid:** Bright colors, clean digital UI, fast motion.

### 20. Monochrome Keynote
- **Best for:** A pitch to a big client, a board update, or the one number that matters in an annual review.
- **Look:** A pure black stage (`#000000`) with nothing on it but the words. White (`#FFFFFF`) for type, mid gray (`#8E8E93`) for secondary lines, and one accent, electric blue (`#2F80FF`), for the key word or number. Headings in **Manrope ExtraBold**, very large and centered. Body in **Manrope Regular**. One idea per scene, never more than 8 words. No icons, no decoration. Numbers appear huge in the center, count up slowly, then hold. Charts are one thin line or one bar. Motion is calm: text fades up from 20px below with a long, smooth ease-out, then a full second of stillness before the next idea. Scenes change with a slow fade through black. Camera holds still, with a rare slow push-in.
- **Avoid:** Clutter, bullet lists, a second accent color.
