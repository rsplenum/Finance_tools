# Gotchas

Traps met in earlier sessions, moved out of `docs/HANDOFF.md` on 04-10-2026 so that what every session reads stays short (D-TECH-29). Grep it for the files and tools you will touch; add one line per new trap.

- **T1's notes** (`engine/data/library/notes.json`, D-UX-34): `tests/library.test.ts` fails on a note over 50 words, one with an amount, a word of safety or "architect", a source not in `sources.json`, or status `own` when a source is outside (or not `own` when all are ours). It also fails when a rate change brings a new family into either V1 case's top five: write that family's notes, then update the list.
- **The planning estimate's answer has a word budget** (V1, D-UX-32): the site check counts `main`'s `innerText` with the answer, before any tap, and fails above 400. Closed `<details>` are not counted, so new detail goes behind a tap, never on the answer; a count must sit apart from its name with a real space, or the two read as one word. A new flag says whether it `decides` (raised by the answers) and the rupees `n` it is about; only those that decide show with the answer.
- **The level's range (L1) is worked only in the package run at the chosen level**: `architect(input, check, true)` from `levelRuns`; other estimates carry no `range`. The engine takes a line's choices by the slot's dimension (`bandOf`, memoised per family, level and units, so the library must not change while the page runs). The check takes those of the same kind of unit as the item named. The library test "every choice at a level …" keeps the two alike: a new item at a level in another unit of count (a set beside nos) blocks the estimate until it is fixed. A line's `at` is the level whose item it takes, capped by the slot (the lights at Premium), and the drawer starts there.
- **The section lines sort by `pkg`, the section's amount in the package**, not by its amount now, so a slider or an item never moves the line being worked on; a section's items are drawn only while its line is open (`onToggle`).
- **Money rounds to the paisa half up, with a millionth of a paisa added, in both computations** (D-TECH-17). A new rounding helper must do the same, or an exact half (a brick's 9 × 1.065 = 9.585) rounds apart in the two and blocks the estimate.
- **A section with `kinds` in `architect.json` is shown only for those kinds** (`sectionFor`): the page lists `e.sections`, never the whole rule book's list.
- **A new house has a stair well on every floor, the ground floor alone too** (the stair to the terrace, E5), and the cabin's 11.25 sq m joins the structure's rules of thumb: Brick&Bolt's per-sq-ft test is on the built-up area plus the cabin.
- **A tank is measured in litres** (the unit `litre`, the dimension `volume`), and an item supplied and fixed with a `pack` has its pack divided out before the city's factor, in `library.ts` and `checkRate` alike. A fixed item with no rate is unpriced and flagged; the library test allows that only with a note saying why.
- **A city's own charge (`byCity`, the sewer connection) is used as it is, never scaled by the city's factor**; a city it does not name takes `rate`, or none, and the line is unpriced there. `price()` takes the city's id as its fifth argument and `checkRate()` as its second: a call without it ignores `byCity`. A change that brings in an unpriced line says so in What changed (`Change.unpriced`).
- **Reading library pages (E2b):** a checked item may cite only sources with `checked` (`tests/library.test.ts` lists every offender in one run). Moglix shows Rs. 15,99x–16,000 as a placeholder on many listings: never a price. IndiaMART's `proddetail` pages refuse the visit (403); its `dir` pages open. An agent's saved file may hold several pages, and its line 1 is not always the address: grep for it. `cp -r` resets the files' times, so take a page's read date from the agent's report.
- **A bathroom list equal to the plan's is left out** (`canon` in `plan-model.ts`), as Medium is, so a new count of bedrooms brings its own bathrooms. A room's × moves every record kept by the later rooms' ids (`moveRooms`): sizes, words, levels, items and brands.
- **When editing a file by script, pass a function to `String.replace`**: a `$` followed by a backquote in the replacement text inserts all the text before the match, and duplicated half of `plan-model.ts` once.
- **A room's size word (R1) is left out of the input at Medium** (`withRoomWord`), and a word puts a typed size aside. The planned sizes depend on the words, so anything that plans the rooms passes them: `planRooms(bhk, carpet, words)` (the page's placeholders too). Each room in Rooms holds its own `details`, so a check opens the Rooms with `:scope > summary`.
- **A room's own size is text in the page's unit in the state, metres in the engine's input** (`inputOf`); a new area unit turns the typed sides (`withUnit`). A side out of range, or one side alone, is not passed on, and the field says why.
- **What changed shows only while the estimate is the one the change made** (`inputOf(state)` equals `change.after`); every `with…` helper notes its change through `noted`, and an answer typed in the six questions makes the line go.
- **Compaction fires at 80% of the window less 20k** (`CLAUDE_CODE_AUTO_COMPACT_WINDOW`, from 100000 to 1000000, capped at the model's window; the variable beats the `autoCompactWindow` setting): at 0.8 × (window − 20000), so 200000 compacted at 147k and 148k in the log and 260000 compacts at about 192k; window − 33k is only the ceiling. The 80% comes from a setting Claude Code can change remotely, so check where compactions fired with the `lessons` skill's `scripts/session-cost.mjs`, not from the code. A session starts at about 60k tokens and is at about 75k after a compaction, so a window below about 170000 compacts every few steps. Without it this model's 1M window never compacts.
- **The cost guard** (`.claude/hooks/cost-guard.mjs`, D-TECH-32) reads the last 2 MB of the session's transcript for the last step's context and time, and takes a new session's start as about 60k (above). Claude Code took up its hooks within the session that added them and runs the file afresh at each call, so an edit acts at once: run `node .claude/hooks/cost-guard.mjs --self-test` (also in `npm run verify`) after each one, since it never fails a session (any error ends it silently). What a hook is handed is not what the session sees: Write and Edit hand it the whole file, a shell command that changed files their diff (`bashEditDiff`), an image its data; it counts none of these. Tokens run about 2.3 characters each here, not 4 (`PER_TOKEN`, measured from the log).
- One session at a time per checkout; check `git log origin/<branch>` and `git status` before any reset.
- The session's git proxy caps concurrent git operations: one clone or fetch at a time.
- Astro runs from inside `site/` (the npm scripts do this): `astro dev --root site` finds no pages in Astro 7.3. The dev server logs to `site/.astro/dev.log`.
- `.astro` files are not type-checked (TypeScript 7): keep logic in `.ts`/`.tsx`.
- Worker runtime types are generated by `npm run typecheck` (or `npm run worker:types`) and gitignored.
- `npm run check:site` needs `npm run build` first. Playwright is pinned to the container's Chromium (1.56.1); if a container ships another version, set `CHROMIUM_PATH=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium`.
- Vitest 5 hides console output from passing tests; `npm run sim` shows the simulation's per-seed summary (about 10 s).
- Stop background servers by PID: `pkill -f 'wrangler dev'` also matches the calling shell.
- The container's LibreOffice is only its core, so it loads no file ("source file could not be loaded"). `apt-get install -y libreoffice-writer libreoffice-calc` adds what it needs, in each new container. Then `soffice --headless -env:UserInstallation=file://<scratch>/lo --convert-to pdf` prints a .docx or .xlsx to a PDF. To look at a PDF page, render it in Node with pdf.js and `@napi-rs/canvas` (in `node_modules`), with `standardFontDataUrl` set to `node_modules/pdfjs-dist/standard_fonts/`.
- `new Blob([bytes])` needs `Uint8Array<ArrayBuffer>` in TypeScript 7: the writers return that type.
- pdf.js in Node: `getDocument(...)` returns a task; call `task.destroy()`, not the document's.
- A field's `change` event (a month picker, or Playwright's `fill`) can arrive before Preact re-renders the draft: commit from the input's own value, as `useDraft` in `site/src/fields.tsx` does. Preact runs `useEffect` only after the next frame (or 35 ms): use `useLayoutEffect` where a field must show a change at once, and make checks wait for what they read (`expectText`, `expectValue`).
- No two files whose names differ only in case (`plan.ts` beside `Plan.tsx`): esbuild fails on them, and macOS and Windows cannot hold both.
- `toLocaleString` with options builds a formatter each call and made the estimate ten times slower: share one `Intl.NumberFormat` (`engine/library.ts`).
- The estimate's questions fold as the sixth is answered, so a check clicks the last radio (`page.click`): `page.check` waits for a radio that is gone.
- Scratch scripts outside the repo cannot import `playwright` by name; import `node_modules/playwright/index.mjs` by path.
- WebFetch goes through the same network policy: rbi.org.in, bcasonline.org and srbatliboi.in were refused, while WebSearch works. Mark anything read only through search results as secondary. WebFetch answers from a small model's summary, never the page's words: take no figure from it (D-TECH-32).
- **Makers' price lists are blocked** (W1, 05-10-2026): this environment's network refused havells.com and every other maker's price-list host tried (CONNECT 403 for curl and the `fetcher`, EGRESS_BLOCKED for WebFetch). Read the MRP from sellers already in `engine/data/sources.json` (moglix.com, industrybuying.com, bestofelectricals.com): bestofelectricals.com/search?q=<range> lists a range's switches, sockets and plates with MRP and price on one page; amazon.in answers 503 and moglix's search returns nothing to a script, or ask the owner to allow the host (`docs/OWNER.md`). Never probe the proxy's status page: the classifier refuses it.
- A row's answers are radios `fld-<key>-<answer>`: a field of that row must not reuse one (the new asset's month is `fld-assetIncome-start`, since `fld-assetIncome-from` is the radio).
- Expected figures in tests: round the exact value once. 6,15,500 ÷ 2,09,000 is 2.94498, which shows as 2.94; rounding 2.9450 again gives a wrong 2.95.
- **A table's columns of words share what the other columns leave** (`wrap` on a `Column`, `site/src/doc/pdf.ts`), at least 40 pt each: give a blank column (Make offered) no `wrap`, or the columns overflow A4 and `measure` moves the last into a second table pages later. A PDF read back wraps a sentence across lines, so a check normalises the spaces before it looks for one.
- The planning estimate's six questions fold into one line once the sixth is answered: count `[data-question]` before answering, and check `pl-summary` after.
- **Brand names** (`brands` and brand-named items in `engine/data/library/*.json`, the chips in `site/src/estimate/PlanEstimate.tsx`, the downloads from `site/src/estimate/plan-document.ts`, any pSEO page) follow `docs/TRADEMARKS.md` (D-BIZ-03): plain text, the generic name first, no logos or product photos, a source and date beside every price, the notice wherever brands show (`BRAND_NOTICE` in `site/src/site.ts`; the site check holds it to rule 4 word for word, D-BIZ-04). The notice shows only where a brand shows: `namesBrand` for a line's item, `drawerBrands` for an open drawer (its levels' open state lives in `Sections`); the bill takes `BRAND_NOTICE_SHORT` (D-BIZ-05).
- **No Node types in the typecheck** (`tsconfig.json` covers `tests/` without `@types/node`): a test importing `node:fs`, or a file through `?raw`, passes vitest but fails `npm run verify`'s typecheck. Read a doc's text in `scripts/site-check.mjs` (plain Node) instead, as TM1 does for the brand notice.
- **Switch MRPs come from Moglix product pages** (W1b, 10-10-2026): bestofelectricals.com's search now answers a script with "No products were found" (its list is built by a script); Moglix's brand listings (`/brands/<brand>/electricals/switches-sockets/{switches/211111100,sockets/211111200,cover-plates/211112517,plates/211112518}?page=N`) open with plain curl and link each product, and each product page shows the price, the GST and the MRP. Its listing shows only the price, sometimes not the product page's. Read the pack in the title (Pack of 10) and divide it out with `pack`. lntsufin.com is refused. Playwright's Chromium fails TLS through the proxy: use curl, and never weaken TLS to load a page.
- **Reading sources from this container** (E2c, 10-10-2026; `engine/data/library/*.json`, `engine/data/sources.json`): the water boards' and corporations' hosts (bwssb.karnataka.gov.in, portal.mcgm.gov.in, pmc.gov.in, delhijalboard.delhi.gov.in, hyderabadwater.gov.in) and techcomm.kohler.com (Kohler's spec sheets) are refused (CONNECT 403) until the owner allows them, except hyderabadwater.gov.in, which opens from E2d but answers a script only at the second identical request (the first gets a 302); Industrybuying lists most switch parts by pack or minimum order without an MRP, so it seldom gives a second seller (E2d); IndiaMART's seller pages answer 429, and wakefit, homecentre, 99acres, chabsons and duroflex refuse; woodage.in answers only to curl's own user-agent (`curl/8.5.0`), any other gets 444, and its hardware bands are illustrative, not quotes; Playwright through the proxy can fail with ERR_CERT_AUTHORITY_INVALID in an agent while working in another (amazon.in, kohler.co.in opened): give it the CA bundle at `/root/.ccr/ca-bundle.crt` or fall back to curl.
- **A city's factor has more digits than it shows** (R2b, 10-10-2026; `tests/architect.test.ts`, `cityFactor` in `engine/architect.ts`): Pune's is 0.915427114…, worked from the cities' ranges, and the rates the page shows are rounded to the paisa only at the end. A hand figure worked with 0.915427 can land on the other side of a half paisa (a tap's 2,481.905 shows 2,481.91): work hand figures with seven digits or more. A share of exactly a half per cent can show either way too (6.25% shows 6.2%), as the shares are worked in sq m.
- **Finding second pages** (E2e, 10-10-2026; `engine/data/library/*.json`): the `fetcher` cannot search, so a Haiku general-purpose agent with WebSearch finds the addresses first (no figures), then the `fetcher` quotes them. Flipkart's product pages give a script the name and pack but no price (`"mrp":0` in its JSON), and Playwright fails on the proxy's certificate there; TradeIndia's product pages print "(Approx.)" beside every price, so they stay leads; TradeIndia and Dial4trade category pages list sellers' per-sq-ft prices without saying whether fixing is in, so they cannot check an installed rate. decure.in, crompton.co.in, comaron.com, mccoymart.com and woodenmax.in open with plain curl. An agent's 'same' on a listing can be a hand-picked line: read the whole list in the saved page before settling.
- **A new quantity rule priced by area must join `AREA_RULES`** (E5b, `engine/architect.ts`): otherwise the engine counts it, and a family priced by the sq ft (the old terrace at `dm-floor`) blocks with "priced by sqft, but … gives count".
- **A one-off vitest script goes in `tests/`, never `--root /`** (met twice, E2d and E5b): the root scan hangs past the timeout. The config silences `console.log`, so write what it prints to a file. And `pkill -f` on the command's own words kills the shell that runs it.
- `site/src/doc/xlsx.ts`, Excel formulas (C1): a `Figure.formula`'s `{ref}` names a row's `ref` on the same part and `{Part!ref}` on another (by the part's name), in the same column, and `{ref@3}` in the table's column 3 (0 the labels; D2, for a total over chosen years); a ref no row carries makes `xlsxOf` throw. So two tables on one part must not share a ref, and the sheets whose cells a formula reads must keep the same year columns. A cell without a figure (a "—") carries no formula.
- vitest with `--root /` (to run a probe test from the scratchpad) crawls the whole disk and hangs past two minutes; put a probe inside `tests/` for one run instead.
- **Tax can rise by more than the profit** (`engine/tax.ts` `taxOn`, D1): in a marginal-relief band (the rebate, or a surcharge's) the tax is capped at the income above the threshold and then 4% cess is added, a marginal rate of 104%. A lower profit can so leave more cash; `tests/dscr.sim.test.ts` bounds a stressed line's rise by the cess on the cut, never assumes it falls.
- **The site check uploads files the tests write** (U1, `scripts/site-check.mjs`): `tests/fixtures/statement-k.pdf` and `statement-k.answer.json` come from `tests/read.test.ts`, so after changing `tests/fixtures/statement-k.ts` or `engine/read.ts` run the tests before the site check (`npm run verify` does). The read section's inputs sit in `#read`, left out of the CMA page's count of 126 default fields; the pdf.js worker is emitted as `.mjs`, so the site check's server maps `.mjs` too.
- **No `@types/node` for the root tsconfig** (U1): a test that writes a file imports `node:fs`, declared alone in `tests/node-fs.d.ts`; widen that file rather than adding node types to the site's code. pdf.js in node is `pdfjs-dist/legacy/build/pdf.mjs` with `verbosity: 0`, else it warns about fonts on every run.
- `worker/` (U1): `wrangler types` types each `vars` value as its literal (`READ_PER_DAY: "50"`), so a test's env is cast; the Worker imports `engine/read.ts`, hence `resolveJsonModule` in `worker/tsconfig.json`. The read caps are KV counters, not atomic: a burst can pass a cap by a few reads, so the spend limit on the Anthropic account is the real ceiling.
- **Make it viable (`engine/viable.ts`, MV2):** a lever's search floor must be a value the report accepts: a term loan of 0 needs no instalments, so the run fails and the lever reads "none"; the floor is Rs. 1 (above PMEGP's margin money). A ratio's target is what meets at the lever's farthest value, so a lever never chases a level it cannot reach. Hand sums for the tests slipped three times (the cash, a threshold, the TL outstanding): work each twice before typing it. The board (MV2b): a lever's `now` read back from the report can carry float dust (6,999,999.999999999 for 70 lakh): test it with `closeTo`; a bar's width is the page's, never a figure shown.
- **The combined plan (`engine/plan.ts`, MV3):** higher sales raise the margin for working capital, so the promoters' money grows with sales and can pass their most after the term loan's cut fitted it: the plan is worked again with the cut less by the excess. A longer term lowers the instalments but the FACR can miss later (the net block falls faster than the loan): the lender's steps scan each value for the fewest that meet the most missed levels without losing one met, never "all met at the far end". A worked figure is tied to its case: D-UX-53's 4,36,226 is case R at 1.2 Cr with a 20 L limit, not at 70 L. The board (`Report.tsx`) closes on any figure but the promoters' most (`base` without it); its sliders' testids are `rep-slider-<lever>`, the bars at the sliders `rep-plan-check-*`, so the bars as typed keep `rep-check-*`.
- **Make it viable on the CMA (`engine/cma-plan.ts`, MV4, D-UX-56):** the DSCR's instalments are the year before's term loans due within 12 months, so the longer-repayment lever moves only the projection's DSCR, never the estimate's (the estimate's service uses the last audited year's figure). Higher sales can lower the current ratio, since stock and debtors grow and the bank's working capital funds the gap: the sales lever is not monotone in every ratio, so search each lever against "meets every level it can", not one ratio. The remaining months assume equal instalments (term loans ÷ due within 12 months × 12). A search that doubles downward must test its floor before bisecting (a fall search stopped at its first halving). The site check counts the fresh-or-renewal radios as one field (`cmaFields`), 127 in all.
- **Make it viable on the DSCR front door (`engine/dscr-plan.ts`, MV5, D-UX-57):** a longer repayment needs years past those shown. `dscrCaseOf` (`site/src/dscr/model.ts`) builds them to the FY of drawdown + 179 months, but only when every later year has a figure: a row typed year by year gives blanks, not needs, from `projFor`, so it falls back to the years shown. Case A's fewest line broke on that once. `withDscrLevers` slices them per trial. The part years count a whole year's cash against fewer EMIs, so DSCR is not monotone in the term: the nearest term when none meets can be 175, not 180. The profit lever is never written to the form. The front door's hints marked by the plan are `#fld-fd-amount-hint` and `#fld-fd-years-hint`. The board's cards show the alone line's sentence (`dscr-text-<id>`), not its move.
- **The document readers in `scripts/read-doc.mjs` need their line in `read-doc.d.mts`** (D1b): a helper used by a test without it passes vitest and fails `npm run verify`'s typecheck. `docxLines` gives `{ lines, messages }`, not an array. A `toMatch` on a whole Excel sheet prints the sheet when it fails: match a short part, and remember `xlsx.ts` always quotes a sheet's name in a formula (`'Sensitivity'!B5`).
- **Review-by dates** (Q2, D-DATA-19; `engine/data/**/*.json`, `scripts/review-dates.mjs`): a data file whose figures are re-checked moves both its `date` and its `reviewBy` (costs 6 months on, tax to its tax year's end, the rest 12 months); verify fails on a `reviewBy` over a year past the `date`. The site check's `open()` takes `at` for another day (its default stays 02-10-2026). `npm run hosts` saying "open" is the front page only: incometaxindia.gov.in then refused the fetcher's pages with a 403 from its edge, and indiacode.nic.in timed out on the Act (12-10-2026).
- **Which part of the Finance Act holds a rate** (`engine/data/tax.json`, D-DATA-21): the rates for the tax year 2026-27 under the Income-tax Act, 2025 are Part III of the First Schedule (firm: Paragraph C; surcharges: Paragraph F), but Part III leaves out the surcharge on income taxed under sections 200 and 202 (the company's concessional rate, the new regime), which section 2(12) of the Finance Act sets. Name the provision from the pages, never from an earlier note: the note said Paragraph B for the firm.
- **Interaction data** (UD1, `site/src/events.ts`, `worker/src/event-shape.ts`): a batch goes as text/plain, since a beacon cannot send a preflight; the Worker parses it. A new event key or kind changes `check()` too, or the Worker refuses every batch (the site check runs each batch through it). Names are element ids: an id built from a typed value would leak it, and five digits in a row drop the event. The site check turns recording on with `window.__EVENTS__`; the build has it off until `PUBLIC_API_URL` is set. Stop `wrangler dev` by its PID: `pkill -f` killed the shell again here.
