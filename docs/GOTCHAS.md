# Gotchas

Traps met in earlier sessions, moved out of `docs/HANDOFF.md` on 04-10-2026 so that what every session reads stays short (D-TECH-29). Grep it for the files and tools you will touch; add one line per new trap.

- **T1's notes** (`engine/data/library/notes.json`, D-UX-34): `tests/library.test.ts` fails on a note over 50 words, one with an amount, a word of safety or "architect", a source not in `sources.json`, or status `own` when a source is outside (or not `own` when all are ours). It also fails when a rate change brings a new family into either V1 case's top five: write that family's notes, then update the list.
- **The planning estimate's answer has a word budget** (V1, D-UX-32): the site check counts `main`'s `innerText` with the answer, before any tap, and fails above 400. Closed `<details>` are not counted, so new detail goes behind a tap, never on the answer; a count must sit apart from its name with a real space, or the two read as one word. A new flag says whether it `decides` (raised by the answers) and the rupees `n` it is about; only those that decide show with the answer.
- **The level's range (L1) is worked only in the package run at the chosen level**: `architect(input, check, true)` from `levelRuns`; other estimates carry no `range`. The engine takes a line's choices by the slot's dimension (`bandOf`, memoised per family, level and units, so the library must not change while the page runs). The check takes those of the same kind of unit as the item named. The library test "every choice at a level …" keeps the two alike: a new item at a level in another unit of count (a set beside nos) blocks the estimate until it is fixed. A line's `at` is the level whose item it takes, capped by the slot (the lights at Premium), and the drawer starts there.
- **The section lines sort by `pkg`, the section's amount in the package**, not by its amount now, so a slider or an item never moves the line being worked on; a section's items are drawn only while its line is open (`onToggle`).
- **Money rounds to the paisa half up, with a millionth of a paisa added, in both computations** (D-TECH-17). A new rounding helper must do the same, or an exact half (a brick's 9 × 1.065 = 9.585) rounds apart in the two and blocks the estimate.
- **A section with `kinds` in `architect.json` is shown only for those kinds** (`sectionFor`): the page lists `e.sections`, never the whole rule book's list.
- **A new house has a stair well on every floor, the ground floor alone too** (the stair to the terrace, E5), and the cabin's 11.25 sq m joins the structure's rules of thumb: Brick&Bolt's per-sq-ft test is on the built-up area plus the cabin.
- **A tank is measured in litres** (the unit `litre`, the dimension `volume`), and an item supplied and fixed with a `pack` has its pack divided out before the city's factor, in `library.ts` and `checkRate` alike. A fixed item with no rate is unpriced and flagged; the library test allows that only with a note saying why.
- **A city's own charge (`byCity`, the sewer connection) is used as it is, never scaled by the city's factor**; a city it does not name takes `rate`, or none, and the line is unpriced there. `price()` takes the city's id as its fifth argument and `checkRate()` as its second: a call without it ignores `byCity`. A change that brings in an unpriced line says so in What changed (`Change.unpriced`).
- **Reading library pages (E2b):** a checked item may cite only sources with `checked` (`tests/library.test.ts` lists every offender in one run). Moglix shows Rs. 15,99x–16,000 as a placeholder on many listings: never a price. IndiaMART's `proddetail` pages refuse the visit (403); its `dir` pages open. An agent's saved file may hold several pages, and its line 1 is not always the address: grep for it. `cp -r` resets the files' times, so take a page's read date from the agent's report.
- **A bathroom list equal to the plan's is left out** (`canon` in `plan-model.ts`), as Medium is, so a new count of bedrooms brings its own bathrooms. A room's × moves every record kept by the later rooms' ids (`moveRooms`): sizes, words, levels, items and brands.
- **When editing a file by script, pass a function to `String.replace`**: a `$` followed by a backquote in the replacement text inserts all the text before the match, and duplicated half of `plan-model.ts` once.
- **A room's size word (R1) is left out of the input at Medium** (`withRoomWord`), and a word puts a typed size aside. The planned sizes depend on the words, so anything that plans the rooms passes them: `planRooms(bhk, carpet, words)` (the page's placeholders too). Each room in Rooms holds its own `details`, so a check opens the Rooms with `:scope > summary`.
- **A room's own size is text in the page's unit in the state, metres in the engine's input** (`inputOf`); a new area unit turns the typed sides (`withUnit`). A side out of range, or one side alone, is not passed on, and the field says why.
- **What changed shows only while the estimate is the one the change made** (`inputOf(state)` equals `change.after`); every `with…` helper notes its change through `noted`, and an answer typed in the six questions makes the line go.
- **Compaction fires at 80% of the window less 20k** (`CLAUDE_CODE_AUTO_COMPACT_WINDOW`, from 100000 to 1000000, capped at the model's window; the variable beats the `autoCompactWindow` setting): at 0.8 × (window − 20000), so 200000 compacted at 147k and 148k in the log and 260000 compacts at about 192k; window − 33k is only the ceiling. The 80% comes from a setting Claude Code can change remotely, so check where compactions fired with the `lessons` skill's `scripts/session-cost.mjs`, not from the code. A session starts at about 60k tokens and is at about 75k after a compaction, so a window below about 170000 compacts every few steps. Without it this model's 1M window never compacts.
- **The cost guard** (`.claude/hooks/cost-guard.mjs`, D-TECH-32) reads the last 2 MB of the session's transcript for the last step's context and time, and takes a new session's start as about 60k (above).
