# Test script: five people, 20 minutes each (UX1, 13-10-2026)

For the owner to run (`docs/ROADMAP.md`, row 4). Five people watched once tell more than any check: where they pause, what they read twice, which word stops them (NN/g, "Why you only need to test with 5 users", 2000). Their notes go to UX2 to UX4 beside the critic's findings (`2026-10-13-*.md` in this folder).

## Whom to ask
Pick five; the rows are examples, not the full set. None may be a customer of your employer bank (D-BIZ-01), and no one brings an office file: everyone works the fictional cards below (D-BIZ-02).

| # | Who | Why them | Tools they try |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A shop owner or proprietor who has taken a business loan | the borrower the DSCR page is for | home, DSCR, estimate |
| 2 | Someone planning to build or renovate a home | the estimate's first reader, likely new to loan words | home, estimate, DSCR |
| 3 | A DSA or loan agent | uses the tools for many clients, on a phone, fast | home, DSCR, project report |
| 4 | A CA or a CA's article assistant | reads CMA and reports for a living; spots wrong terms | home, CMA, project report |
| 5 | A small manufacturer, or their accountant | renews a working-capital limit each year | home, CMA, DSCR |

Ask each on their own phone if they can (most will open it on Android at about 360 px); one of the five on a laptop.

## Before (2 minutes)
Say, in your words: "I am testing the site, not you. Nothing you do is wrong. Please think aloud: say what you look at, what you expect, what puzzles you. I will not help, but you can stop any time." Open https://finance-tools-9if.pages.dev on their device. Start a timer per task.

## The tasks (16 minutes; four tasks of about four minutes)
Read each task aloud and hand over its card. Do not name a button, a field or a menu: the task says what they want, never where to click.

**T1, home (1 minute).** "You have a loan question. Which tool here would you open first, and why?" Note: what they read first, whether each line says what the tool gives.

**T2, DSCR (4 minutes).** Card: "Asha Traders, a firm. Profit last year Rs. 4.5 lakh, growing 0% a year. Wants a loan of Rs. 12 lakh at 12% for 5 years." Ask: "Can the business repay this loan? What would make it work?" Note: did they find the verdict without scrolling; did they find and use Make it viable; did they understand "DSCR".
Then: "Change the profit growth to 10%. Did the answer change?"

**T3, estimate (4 minutes).** Card: "A 2 BHK flat of 1,000 sq ft carpet area in Pune; you want to renovate at a basic level." Ask: "About how much will it cost? Which part costs most?" Then: "Make the flooring dearer and see what happens." Note: the six questions, the level strip, whether they open a section.

**T4, project report (4 minutes).** Card: "A firm starting a unit. Building Rs. 10 L, machinery Rs. 20 L, furniture Rs. 2 L, preliminary Rs. 1 L. Term loan Rs. 25 L at 10%, 60 monthly instalments from April 2026. Sales Rs. 1.2 Cr a year growing 10%; materials 65% of sales; fixed costs Rs. 18 L growing 5%; a working-capital limit of Rs. 5 L at 11%." Ask: "Get the project report a lender would want, and tell me whether the lender would be satisfied." Note: More options (sales sit there), the status line, where they look for the answer.

**T5, CMA (4 minutes).** Ask: "Use the worked example. What limit does it support? Then download the CMA for 'K Steel Furniture Pvt Ltd'." Note: whether they find the example button, the limit, the forms, the name field and the download.

Each person does T1 and the three tasks of their row; if time runs short, stop the task, not the session.

## After (2 minutes)
Ask: "What was the hardest part? One word you did not understand? Would you use this again, or send it to someone? What would you pay for it?" Thank them.
