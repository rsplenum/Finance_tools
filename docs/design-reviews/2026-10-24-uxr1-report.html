UXR1 · Review of the four tools · 24-10-2026

So simple to be difficult

“It is so simple to be difficult but so difficult to be simple.”

This review covers the DSCR, CMA, project report and construction estimate pages, built locally from main at 45602f0. They were checked in every state, at 320, 360 and 1280 px, in light and dark, by keyboard, on a slowed phone, after a reload, and in every file they download. The terms of reference are in docs/design-reviews/2026-10-24-uxr1-terms.html. Figures were compared only with what the engine printed; I computed none.

The verdict

The craft is sound: 0 measured defects on all four pages, no sideways scroll at 320 px, results and errors announced to screen readers, one filled button per screen, lakh and crore throughout, and complete downloads. What makes the tools difficult is not any one screen. It is what happens between screens and between tools:

  • The work does not survive. A reload, the back button, or a phone discarding the tab wipes every answer (one blocker).
  • The answer is said too often, and too late. The figure appears two to four times on screen, but on a phone the report and the CMA put it, or its download, after many screens of questions.
  • Four tools, four grammars. Each tool has its own words for a verdict, its own way to ask for more, its own place for Clear all, and its own set of downloads. The DSCR tool also uses a different lowest-year target from the other two (1.20 against 1.25).

None of this needs new maths. Most of the fixes take something away: a repeated figure, a heading, a line before the first question, or a second word for the same thing. That is the difficult part of being simple.

DSCR

Nearest to simple

Eight questions; the answer is 2.1 screens down. It falls short in three places: an expert's way out comes before question 1, “meets the target” is printed on all six year rows, and the year cards run 4.3 screens before the download.

CMA

Simple to read, difficult to finish

128 fields on one phone page, 14,198 px tall, with the download 17.2 screens down. A detour (“Or read your audited statement”) sits above the default path. 104 words come before the first question, against an aim of 40.

Project report

Says “simple”, asks for more

It promises “About a dozen questions”, then shows 16 and says 13 are still to answer. On a phone the answer is 4.8 screens down. The page says Complete while the document says Provisional.

Estimate

Quickest, heaviest

The answer is 0.9 screens down, but the headline gives three unlabelled amounts, and the same figure appears three ways (8.4 lakh, 8,40,271, 8.40). Its scripts weigh 581 kB, so it starts in 4.35 s on a slowed phone. It has no Clear all.

How findings are ranked

Findings are ranked first by severity (the terms of reference §5). Within each grade they are ranked by score = Difficulty × Fix, highest first:

  • Difficulty: 3 = it stops a task or gives a wrong reading; 2 = it slows or confuses; 1 = it is friction.
  • Fix: 3 = a change of words or data, S; 2 = one component, S–M; 1 = a change of structure, M–L.

A score of 9 means very difficult and cheap to fix, so do it first.

Where this touches the explainer videos

The 🎬 findings change what is on screen: the verdict words (S5), the band repeating the card (S6), the line under each heading (S13), the “Tool N of 4” eyebrow (P3) and the estimate's h1 (P5). The CMA and report layouts (S3, S9, S20) change the scrolling shots. A scene recorded now, from VID2a or VID2b, would show screens that UXR3–UXR6 then change.

Recommendation: keep the voice-over and storyboards, and record the screens after UXR3 and UXR4 merge. Both are cheap. I did not read or touch VID2's branch, PR #116, or its files.

Tradeoffs for the owner

  • sessionStorage or localStorage. sessionStorage survives a reload and a discarded tab, but not closing the tab; it is the safer default on a shared office computer. localStorage keeps the work for days, but needs a visible “Forget my answers” control. I recommend sessionStorage.
  • 1.20 or 1.25. One value is simplest. If lenders really differ, name the norm in the result rather than keep two silent targets. I recommend one value, with the lender norm as an assumption the user can change.
  • Order of the rows. UXR2 removes the only blocker. UXR3 and UXR4 are cheap, and they fix the video screens. UXR6 is the one L. I recommend UXR2 → UXR3 → UXR4 → UXR5 → UXR6 → UXR7.
  • Hiding the band. It shows the answer only once, but a user who scrolls back up loses sight of the figure for a moment. I recommend hiding it anyway: the card is on screen in that moment.
  • A worked example on every tool. It makes each tool easy to try, but it adds one button above the questions. I recommend a text link, not a button, so that “Download PDF” stays the only filled button.

What already works — keep it

  • 0 measured defects on all four pages, and an axe-clean site check.
  • No sideways scroll at 320 px and no console errors.
  • Live regions announce results and errors, and every error sets aria-invalid with a message.
  • One filled button per screen.
  • Lakh and crore are used throughout.
  • Home's fictional case opens in each tool and is marked as fictional.
  • The downloads are complete, and the Excel files carry a Check sheet.

Evidence scripts and their outputs stayed in the session scratchpad: walk.mjs, walk2.mjs, walk3.mjs, docs.mjs, dr-*.txt, load.txt, and the critic's m/ folder. The writer and critic agents' reports are folded into the findings above.
