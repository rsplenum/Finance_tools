Make it work did not work. Here is why, and the rebuild.

I typed a case shaped like yours into the live builder, walked every step at a phone's size and ran the engine behind it. Below: what is broken, the new screens to tap through, and the three rows that build them.

What I found

Four causes sit behind "Make it work does not work". None of them is the look; all of them are fixable.

1

It tries one change at a time

Your case needs two or three changes together. So every card ends "leaves fixed-asset cover short" and the page tells you to combine them yourself.

2

The part that combines them was switched off

The engine can already work a combined plan. Since 1 November the page opens eight sliders at "No slider moved yet" instead. You were made the solver.

3

One check can never pass

Interest cover is judged on the first, part year: a few months of sales against the interest. I ran the engine's own solver and 972 more trials; nothing a borrower changes mends it. This one is yours to decide, below.

4

A hidden assumption sinks the late years

Prices are held flat while costs rise each year. That is why your DSCR slides from 2.48 to 0.58. The page never says so; it only offers "price growth 1.2%" as one card among many.

    Yours to decide: one question

    Should the first, part year be judged?

    Today interest cover is judged in the year sales begin, even when that year holds only a few months of sales. On your screen that is the −0.84 in 2027-28. No plan can mend it, so no plan can pass.

    My pick: judge interest cover from the first full year of sales, and print the part year marked "part year, not judged". Cash running short in that year stays judged. Say yes or no; FIX1 builds on your answer.
