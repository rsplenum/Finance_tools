## Tradeoffs for the owner

1. **Ads or the no-cookies promise.** AdSense's ads set cookies. (a) Personalised ads with consent: the most revenue, but the footer's "No cookies." goes and the consent banner appears in Europe. (b) Ads only on the guide pages, with the promise narrowed to "the tools set no cookies; the guide pages carry Google's ads, which do". (c) No ads until traffic pays for the change. *Recommended: (b).* The tools keep their promise, and the pages that earn are the ones search brings.
2. **Where the slot sits.** One slot per guide page, after its text and below the worked case, never beside the tool's button or download row. No ads on home, the tools, the policies or the 404. *Recommended*, and it follows D-BIZ-11 and A8.
3. **Consent in Europe.** Google's own CMP (free, set up in AdSense, no code) or ads shown only in India. *Recommended: Google's CMP.* Visitors from abroad are few now, but the vision takes the site abroad.
4. **When to apply.** Review takes "a few days … 2-4 weeks" (12169212), and a site of 23 thin pages risks a "low-value content" refusal. *Recommended:* apply at LAUNCH as `docs/ROADMAP.md` has it, after SEO4's 60 or so pages, not at SEO2.
5. **An About page.** Not required by any rule found, but reviewers and borrowers look for who runs a site. *Recommended:* a short one in SEO2 (who runs it, how the figures are worked, the sources), linked from the footer.
