## Gotchas
- **An EMI and a stepped instalment are worked monthly only** (`engine/dscr.ts`, `engine/repayment.ts`, D-UX-198): quarterly instalments have one way, equal principal, and the card says so; a stepped loan typed where no step fits (instalments within a year) is worked as an EMI.
- **The ways to repay run the report three times** (`repaymentView`, D-UX-198): the card is worked only while the answer is shown (`p.view`), and fixOf's hundred runs never call it.
- **A firm's surcharge above Rs. 1 crore of income gives a marginal tax near 74% just past it** (marginal relief, `engine/tax.ts`): right by the slabs, but a case near that line moves its DSCR oddly between the ways to repay. Keep test cases clear of it.
- **A rule read from an open PR** (CLAUDE.md, D-TECH-69): the context can hold a copy of CLAUDE.md from a branch not yet merged (OR5 met D-TECH-70's "no notes, no fold" from PR #133); before writing the shared docs, read the rule from `git show origin/main:CLAUDE.md`.
