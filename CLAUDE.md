# Working on this repo (for AI agents and developers)

- Start with `docs/HANDOFF.md` (the state and the Next table) and grep `docs/GOTCHAS.md` for the files you will touch, then only the parts of `docs/DECISIONS.md` you need (indexed). `docs/PROJECT.md` has the what/why. `docs/LESSONS-CARRIED.md` holds the lessons from the owner's earlier project: read §1 and §8 once per session.
- Keep token use low (owner's request): grep before reading, text assertions over screenshots, independent commands together in one step, many pages through one script that prints only what is needed, since every step re-reads the whole context (D-TECH-23); end each session by HANDOFF's list (lessons, the next row, a starter prompt).
- Every session has one deliverable and a budget set before it starts (owner's request, D-TECH-29): its row in `docs/HANDOFF.md` names what the owner will open or download, its size (S, M or L) and its plan, and preparing the next row is part of the session that ends. Stop at the budget plus a quarter and hand off the rest; `npm run cost` gives the actual for HANDOFF's table. New work starts in a new session; a quick ask may stay while the cache is warm. On a cost guard warning (D-TECH-32) tell the owner once and follow their word; on a big result, print less next time. A file every session reads whole stays short, its size capped by a check.
- Sessions (owner's request, D-TECH-62): one per row, started only from the owner's main conversation, three at most at once (more hit the usage limit). A new one pushes its row's branch first: the claim. A branch and its PR are its maker's; another session only reads and reports until the owner hands it over. A hook guards the first two. A row writes the shared docs only via its notes, `docs/rows/<ROW>.md`, folded after merge (D-TECH-66).
- Gears (owner's request, D-TECH-26): a session starts at the effort its row in `docs/HANDOFF.md` names, in the app's words: high, or Extra where its main work is engine maths or design. Max is for bursts the owner switches on for a hard patch (a skill cannot change it, E2). Within the effort, think briefly on docs, bookkeeping and regenerated files, and fully on money maths, a red check or a design choice. A change of effort keeps the cache on the model these sessions run, and a change of model does not, so the model stays as started. Hand an errand that reads much new material and returns little, such as fetching sources, to the `fetcher` agent; never a figure, engine code, a design or a fix. The fetcher runs on Sonnet; a batch that takes no figure (which pages open, addresses, names) may run it on Haiku (`model: "haiku"`). WebFetch answers from a small model's summary, so never take a figure from it. Ultracode stays off: it spends more, not less.
- Websites (owner's request, D-TECH-35): before a session reads any website, by curl, a browser or the `fetcher`, list the hosts it needs, run `npm run hosts -- <hosts>` once, and give the owner the blocked ones to allow before the work starts; never try a blocked host, nor send the fetcher to one. Hosts still blocked: `docs/OWNER.md`.
- Answers stand (owner's request, D-TECH-39): once given, an answer is final. Spend each new turn on the new ask; revisit an earlier one only when the owner points out a problem. An error met by chance gets one line, never a review.
- Whenever the owner asks for something, give the tradeoffs they may not have considered: the three to five that could change the decision, each with a recommendation (owner, 30-09-2026).
- Skills in `.claude/skills/` load when relevant: `brief-first` (anything the owner asks to build or change), `money-maths-checks` (any figure), `lender-documents` (downloads and printouts), `lessons` (the end of each session, a correction, redone work, a failure that could recur). General copies in `portable/` go to the owner's claude.ai account for every project, and a lesson that holds anywhere goes there too (D-TECH-27).
- Numbers come only from the deterministic engine (`engine/`, pure TypeScript, no DOM). Never let an AI compute amounts, ratios, eligibility or prices shown to users. AI may draft narrative text from the user's own inputs, marked as a draft. As an option beside typing, AI may read figures from the borrower's own documents, each shown with its page and line, cross-checked by the engine and confirmed by the borrower before it counts (D-UX-44).
- Rules, benchmarks and rates live only in dated data files under `engine/data/`, each value with its source and date; lender-specific values are marked unverified until confirmed.
- Never default a missing fact: show what is needed and keep the result provisional. The only exceptions are the assumptions in `engine/data/defaults.json` (owner's request, D-UX-08) and the architect's rules in `engine/data/architect.json` (owner's request, D-UX-18), each listed on the page with its reason until changed (on the planning estimate, behind one line with their count: the owner's answer, D-UX-32).
- Test against worked figures from outside the model: the owner's own fictional cases worked at home, or public worked examples with their source. Never anything from the owner's office or employer, not even anonymised (D-BIZ-02). Every quoted figure has an independent second computation.
- Screens use plain words; lists the owner gives are examples, not the full set; where rules are silent, flag, do not restrict.
- A page's default path asks only the inputs the owner listed for it; that list is a ceiling, not an example. For the DSCR front door: the owner's seven inputs, who the borrower is among them, with depreciation as an optional eighth (D-UX-10). Everything else is an assumption stated on the page or sits under one closed "More options"; from P1f the site check counts the default fields and fails if the count grows (D-UX-12).
- Every change: `npm run verify -- --quick` (0 violations; the site check on touched pages), a line per decision in the row's notes, commit; draft PR, done when CI's full check is green (D-TECH-68); the owner says "merge".
- No bank-internal material, circulars or customer data in this repo. Sample data anonymised; secrets only in environment settings.
- Legal condition (D-BIZ-01): market across India on the internet, but never sell directly to the customers of the owner's employer bank. No pages, campaigns or outreach aimed at them, and none of that bank's material.
- The estimate works "as an architect would", but never calls the tool, a page or a document an architect: the Architects Act 1972, s. 37, keeps the title to registered architects (D-UX-18).
- Build for Cloudflare Pages + Workers from day one (D-TECH-02); every branch gets a preview deploy.

## Compact Instructions
When compacting, keep: the owner's latest request in their words; the branch, the PR's number and state, and what is committed, pushed or only on disk; decisions, figures and errors met this session that are not yet in `docs/`; the next step. Leave out what a new session reads anyway: CLAUDE.md, the skills and `docs/` (name the file), file contents and tool output (name the path), and finished work (give the commit).
