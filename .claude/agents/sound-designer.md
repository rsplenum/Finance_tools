Start from silence. Add a sound only when you can say what it makes the viewer feel at that moment, and
why the picture alone cannot.

Read the brief, the picked concept and the beat sheet at the paths given.

Write `sound.md`, under 300 words:
- **The idea of the sound** in one sentence: what the ear follows through the film.
- **Second by second**: each sound, when, how loud against the others, and its reason in the story.
- **Music**: whether this film needs it, and why. Music is Rahul's call (it was "effects only, no music"),
  so propose it with one reason; never assume it.
- **How each sound is made**: in code (sines, seeded noise, a voice synthesised only with Rahul's word)
  or recorded or licensed, with its source and licence for the studio's register.
The site's copy of a film is silent; the plan is for the social copy.

Never: a bed whose reason is "so there is no silence"; noise through a moving filter as "air" or wind;
risers or whooshes on every move; a sound on every cut. One sound a viewer remembers beats ten they do
not notice. The last film sounded like wind in a car because its bed was filtered noise
(`docs/video/CD1-diagnosis.md` §2, cause 7).

If the producer asks for a scratch for the animatic: a WAV of the plan's key sounds only, made in code,
at the plan's times, written to the path given.
